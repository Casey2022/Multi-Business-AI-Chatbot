# Crosstown Pizza Co. — Menu & Policies

## Pizza Sizes and Prices (Topics: pizza, size, small, medium, large, price)
Our pizzas come in a 10-inch personal, a 14-inch medium and an 18-inch
large, plus a 16x16 Sicilian square pan, which takes an extra ten minutes
in the oven. Gluten-free crust comes in the 10-inch size only; we can't
make a medium or large gluten-free.

## Toppings (Topics: toppings, pepperoni, veggie, meat, cheese)
Vegan cheese is available. We do not have a vegan crust — the standard
dough contains no dairy or egg, but it is made on shared equipment.

## Wings (Topics: wings, bone in, boneless, sauce, heat)
Bone-in or boneless, same price. Sauces: mild, medium, hot, suicidal,
BBQ, honey garlic, garlic parm, lemon pepper. Wings come with celery and
your choice of blue cheese or ranch. Forty-piece orders add about ten
minutes.

## Subs and Salads (Topics: sub, hoagie, sandwich, salad, lunch)
Subs come on a 10-inch roll with lettuce, tomato, onion and our house
dressing: steak, chicken finger, meatball, Italian, turkey, veggie.
Salads: garden, with grilled chicken, or antipasto. Dressings: ranch,
blue cheese, Italian, balsamic, Greek.

## Party Trays (Topics: party tray, catering, large order, office)
Party trays feed 15 to 20: a half-sheet pizza tray, a 50-wing tray, or a
sub platter. On Friday and Saturday nights trays have an order-by time,
because the oven is full after that. The cutoff is for trays only: past
it, regular pizzas, wings and subs can still be ordered for tonight
right up until the kitchen stops taking orders. Trays tie up the kitchen
for about 45 minutes, which is why they're booked as their own slot.

## Delivery (Topics: delivery, deliver, how far, fee, minimum)
We deliver within 6 miles of the Culver Rd shop. The delivery fee is
$3 and the minimum order is $15. Delivery normally takes 30 to 45
minutes, and 45 to 70 on Friday and Saturday evenings. Outside the
6-mile zone we can't deliver, but pickup at the counter is always
available with no minimum.

## Pickup (Topics: pickup, collect, counter, call ahead)
Pickup orders are placed by phone at (585) 555-0177 and are usually
ready in 20 minutes. There's no minimum and no fee. This chat is set
up for delivery orders, so for pickup, give the shop a ring.

## Hours and Busy Times (Topics: hours, open, closed, busy, wait)
The kitchen stops taking orders at 9:45pm. Friday and Saturday between
6pm and 8pm is the busiest stretch of the week and everything takes
longer then — order early if you're feeding a crowd.

## Allergens (Topics: allergy, allergen, gluten, nut, dairy)
Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, soy, and fish (anchovies) all day,
on shared surfaces and shared equipment. Gluten-free crust is made
from a sealed mix but baked in the same oven, so we cannot call any
item allergen-safe. Anyone with a serious allergy should call the shop
and speak to the kitchen directly before ordering.

## Payment (Topics: payment, card, cash, tip, contactless)
We take all major cards, cash, and contactless, in the shop and at the
door. Tips for drivers can go on the card or in cash. We don't split
payment across more than two cards, which is a point of some
contention on Friday nights.
