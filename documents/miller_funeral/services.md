## Cremation Packages (Topics: cremation, cremation cost, simple cremation, direct cremation, memorial, visitation, viewing)
Cremation package prices are the funeral home's charges only; charges
paid to others (such as the crematory fee and death certificates) are
added. Every cremation package includes transfer of the deceased,
professional services, refrigeration for 36 hours, a cremation
container, transportation to the crematory, and return of the cremated
remains to the funeral home in a container the crematory provides.
Simple Cremation is those items only, with no visitation and no service.
Cremation with Memorial Visitation adds 2 hours of visitation at the
funeral home. Cremation with Visitation and Service adds 2 hours of
visitation and a memorial service in our interfaith chapel on the same
day. Traditional Visitation with cremation adds 2 hours of visitation
(a viewing), a same-day memorial service in our chapel, embalming, and a
rental casket for the viewing. Cremation with Private Visitation is also
offered.

## Burial Packages (Topics: burial, funeral cost, traditional funeral, cemetery, interment, casket)
Burial package prices are the funeral home's charges only. The casket
is chosen separately and is not included in any burial package, and
charges paid to others (such as cemetery charges) are added. Every
burial package includes transfer of the deceased, professional
services, refrigeration for 36 hours, staff for the cemetery service,
and transportation of your loved one to the cemetery. Cemetery Service
is a service at the cemetery, with no embalming and no visitation.
Visitation and Interment at Cemetery adds embalming and 2 hours of
visitation at the funeral home. Visitation and Service in Our
Interfaith Chapel, with Interment at Cemetery, adds embalming, 2 hours
of visitation, and a funeral service at the funeral home.

## Charges Paid to Others (Topics: third-party charges, crematory fee, death certificates, obituary notice, newspaper, officiant, church, cemetery charges)
These are not funeral home charges. New York State law requires them to
be charged to the family at exactly the rate the third party charges.
Certified death certificates cost the same whether we obtain them or the
family gets them from the county registrar. A paid death notice is an
obituary printed in a newspaper. Cemetery charges vary by cemetery; for
cremation, they apply only if the cremated remains are buried in a
cemetery.

## Pre-Planning (Topics: pre-planning, preplanning, plan ahead, prearrange, consultation, free consultation, change my plan)
Planning ahead spares your family about 70 to 75 decisions in the first
24 to 48 hours after a death, and makes sure your wishes are followed.
You can change your plan at any time. You can decide in advance on
burial or cremation, casket or urn, and the kind of service (religious,
military or non-traditional). We create a written plan of every choice,
down to pallbearers, clothing, music and speakers. There is no charge
for a pre-planning consultation and no fee to keep a pre-paid account.

## Paying in Advance and the Miller Guarantee (Topics: prepay, pre-paid, prepaid funeral, guarantee, price freeze, move away, refund, Medicaid, spend-down)
If you pay 100% of the estimated funeral home charges when the account
is opened, Miller guarantees no more money will be needed for those
funeral home charges, as long as nothing is added to the plan later.
The guarantee covers funeral home charges only, not charges paid to
others. Pre-paid money stays in your name and never becomes the funeral
home's property. If you move away, the funeral home changes ownership,
or you choose another provider, 100% of the money and 100% of the
interest it has earned go with you. We can help with Medicaid planning
and the spend-down process, so that money set aside for a funeral is
protected.
