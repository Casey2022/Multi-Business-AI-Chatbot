# Ridgeline Contracting — Services & Policies

## How Estimates Work (Topics: estimate, quote, free, visit, how it works)
Estimates are free and carry no obligation. An estimator visits the
property, walks the space with you, takes measurements and photos, and
sends a written quote within three business days. Most estimate visits
take an hour; whole-room remodels take about ninety minutes. We book
estimates two to five business days out, sooner if we have a
cancellation.

## Kitchen Remodels (Topics: kitchen, cabinets, countertops, island)
Past kitchen projects have run from $18,000 for a cabinet-and-counter
refresh to $75,000 for a full gut with layout changes. What moves the
number most is whether plumbing or electrical has to move, and whether
the cabinets are stock, semi-custom, or custom. A typical kitchen takes
five to eight weeks on site once materials arrive. These are ranges
from past work, not a quote — every kitchen is priced after a visit.

## Bathroom Remodels (Topics: bathroom, tub, shower, tile, vanity)
Past bathroom projects have run from $12,000 for a tub-to-shower
conversion to $38,000 for a full primary bath. Older homes in the
city often need the subfloor replaced once the old tile comes up,
which we can't see until demolition — the estimator will flag it as a
possibility when they visit. A typical bathroom takes three to five
weeks.

## Decks and Porches (Topics: deck, porch, railing, composite, pressure treated)
Pressure-treated decks have run $35 to $50 per square foot; composite
$55 to $80. A permit is required in most towns we work in, and we pull
it. Deck season books out — enquiries in March are usually building in
June. We don't build over existing structures without inspecting the
footings first.

## Basement Finishing (Topics: basement, egress, ceiling, moisture)
Finished basements have run $35,000 to $70,000 depending on ceiling
height, egress requirements, and whether a bathroom is going in. We
will not finish a basement with an active water problem — the estimator
checks for it, and if there's evidence we'll tell you what has to
happen first, even if that means no job for us.

## Roofing and Siding (Topics: roof, shingles, siding, gutters)
Asphalt shingle roofs have run $9,000 to $22,000 depending on pitch,
square footage, and how many layers come off. Vinyl siding has run
$14,000 to $30,000. Both are weather-dependent and we won't start a
tear-off with bad weather in the forecast.

## General Repairs (Topics: repair, small job, door, drywall, handyman)
We take smaller repairs — doors, drywall, trim, rot, storm damage —
between larger projects. Repair quotes are a shorter visit, about
thirty minutes. Minimum call-out for repair work is $350.

## Licensing, Insurance and Permits (Topics: licensed, insured, permit, workers comp)
Fully licensed and insured in New York State. We carry general
liability and workers' compensation on every crew, and certificates
are available on request. We pull permits for any work that needs one
and the cost is itemised separately in the quote — we never fold it
into a lump sum.

## Payment and Change Orders (Topics: payment, deposit, schedule, change order)
Projects are invoiced in stages: a deposit at contract signing,
progress payments at agreed milestones, and a final payment at
completion and walk-through. We do not ask for the full amount up
front, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Any change to the
agreed scope is priced and signed as a written change order before the
work happens — no surprises on the final invoice.

## Service Area and Travel (Topics: service area, how far, travel, towns)
We work across Monroe County and the surrounding towns, within about
35 miles of the Lyell Ave shop. For larger projects we'll travel
further — call and ask. Smaller repair work we keep close to home,
because a two-hour round trip for a door is not fair to the customer.

## Warranty (Topics: warranty, guarantee, workmanship, callback)
Our workmanship is warrantied for two years from completion.
Manufacturer warranties on materials pass through to you and vary by
product — they're listed in the quote. If something we built fails
inside the warranty, we come back and fix it; call (585) 555-0166 and say it's
a warranty callback.
