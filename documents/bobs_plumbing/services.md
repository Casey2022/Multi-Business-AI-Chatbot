# Bob's Plumbing — Services Guide

## Emergency Service
We offer 24/7 emergency plumbing service throughout greater Rochester. After
business hours, calls to (585) 555-0123 connect directly to our on-call
dispatcher, who can typically have a technician on-site within 90 minutes
for emergencies in the Greater Rochester area. There is a $75 after-hours
service call fee in addition to standard labor rates.

## Drain Cleaning
We clear all types of drains: kitchen sinks, bathroom sinks, tubs, showers,
toilets, and main sewer lines. Standard drain cleaning is $150 for the first
hour and $75 per additional hour. We use cable augers for most clogs and
hydro-jetting for stubborn or recurring blockages. Hydro-jetting is $400
for a typical home service line.

## Leak Repair
Visible leaks under sinks, around toilets, or at exposed pipe joints can
usually be repaired same-day. Hidden leaks (inside walls, slab leaks)
require detection equipment and may take 1-2 days to fully diagnose and
repair. We charge $125 for the initial leak detection visit, which is
applied to the repair cost if you proceed.

## Water Heater Installation
We install gas, electric, and tankless water heaters from major brands
including Rheem, Bradford White, and Navien. A standard 50-gallon tank
installation runs $1,800-$2,400 depending on the unit. Tankless installation
ranges from $3,500-$5,500. Installation includes haul-away of the old unit
and a 10-year warranty on parts and labor.

## Service Area
We service homes and small businesses within a 20-mile radius of Rochester,
NY. This includes Webster, Penfield, Fairport, Pittsford, Brighton,
Henrietta, Greece, and Irondequoit. Outside that radius, we may still take
the job but charge a travel fee starting at $50.

## Brands We Service
We work on all major brands including Kohler, American Standard, Delta,
Moen, Rheem, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai, and most others. For warranty
work on a specific brand, please mention it when you call so we can ensure
we have the right replacement parts.

## Septic Tanks, Well Drilling, and Commercial Work (Not Offered)
We do not handle septic tank pumping, well drilling, or commercial
industrial plumbing projects. If you ask about septic systems, well work,
or commercial plumbing, we can recommend trusted partners in the area —
just call and ask.
